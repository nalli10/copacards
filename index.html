Partner settings

Default for new shows. Each show can override its split independently.

Card valuation defaults
When a card is used as a prize, this % of its market value is booked as the show's prize-cost expense, and that amount is paid to the card's owner. e.g. 85% of a $200 card = $170 prize cost.
Reimbursements & adjustments
When a partner uses their personal card to bid on shows, those transactions show up as buyer @username on Sales. The full bid price is treated as a per-show expense (reduces profit, both partners share Whatnot's fee proportionally) and 100% routes back to the partner who paid. Blank out the buyer username when you switch to a company card.

Prior work fee is a one-time lump sum owed to P1 for setup work before the partnership. Adds to P1's total on the Dashboard. Reduce this number when paid back.

Cutoff date: from this date forward, the reimbursement buyer's bids count as ordinary partnership expenses only — no partner reimbursement. Past sales (before this date) still route back to the configured partner. Leave blank to disable the cutoff entirely.
